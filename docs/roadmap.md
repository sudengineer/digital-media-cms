# Product Roadmap

> Indicative roadmap for a fictional product. Timing and scope are planning assumptions, not delivery commitments.

## Roadmap Principles
- Deliver an end-to-end workflow before adding breadth.
- Prioritize governance and reliability for externally published content.
- Validate usability with representative editorial users at each release.
- Avoid treating roadmap dates as guarantees before dependency discovery.

## Now / MVP — Core Editorial Workflow
**Outcome:** An editorial team can create, review, approve, schedule, and publish articles safely.

- Article draft and editing
- Metadata and taxonomy basics
- Review and approval workflow
- RBAC and audit events
- Immediate and scheduled publishing
- Basic search and status views
- UAT, monitoring, operational runbook

**Exit signals:** End-to-end UAT passes; no unresolved critical security defects; publishing failure is observable and recoverable.

## Next — Operational Efficiency
**Outcome:** Reduce editorial coordination and improve visibility.

- Editorial dashboard and queue management
- Advanced filtering and saved views
- Better version comparison
- SEO validation improvements
- Bulk metadata operations with safeguards
- Workflow notifications and reminders

**Exit signals:** Improved review cycle time and metadata completeness versus measured baseline.

## Later — Multi-Format and Scale
**Outcome:** Support broader content operations and channel needs.

- Video, audio, and gallery-specific fields
- Localization workflow
- Expanded channel configuration
- Advanced taxonomy governance
- Operational analytics and content performance integration

**Exit signals:** New content types meet workflow, quality, and reliability standards.

## Prioritization Framework
Use a blended approach:
- **RICE** for comparable opportunities: Reach × Impact × Confidence ÷ Effort.
- **Risk reduction** for security, compliance, and publishing reliability.
- **Dependency sequencing** for platform capabilities that unblock multiple features.

Do not use a numeric score as a substitute for product judgment. Record assumptions and confidence alongside scores.
